# Code of conduct

Keygrid is built for engineers and artists at every level, from first bedroom setups to professional studios. Everyone who takes part in this project's issues, discussions and other spaces is expected to help keep it welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to audio or to GitHub.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to others.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing conversations.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Posting copyrighted music or other material you don't have the right to share.
- Spam or unrelated promotion.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, or block people who don't follow this code of conduct.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on [@ChrisCollins24's GitHub profile](https://github.com/ChrisCollins24). Reports are kept confidential.

This code of conduct applies to all Keygrid project spaces, including this repository's issues and discussions.
